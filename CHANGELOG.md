# Changelog

All notable changes to Pro Notes are documented in this file.

## v18.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### What's changed

- **S Pen Pressure Sensitivity & Stylus Fixes:** Fixed Samsung S Pen pressure normalization (no longer crushed by raw digitizer range reporting) and added true hardware pressure-to-width dynamics across Fountain, Ball, Brush, Lettering, Pencil, Crayon, and Calligraphy pens; fixed Hold-to-Erase on older Samsung tablets (e.g., Galaxy Tab S7+) with expanded S Pen side-button detection and 420ms hover-to-touch latching.
- **Interactive Tables (Notebooks & PDFs):** Added a first-class Table tool (`+` Insert menu & Pen Quick Actions) to place customizable tables on any notebook or PDF page, edit any cell directly with the keyboard (with cell navigation, add/remove rows & columns, header row toggle, and color themes), and write or highlight freely on top of cells.
- **Live Interactive Web Pages:** Insert a live, movable, resizable website directly inside any note page — scroll and interact inside the webpage without panning your note, navigate Back/Forward/Reload, or collapse to a link card.
- **Adaptive 3D Page Curl (Reading Mode):** Upgraded the book reader page curl to adapt naturally to your drag direction and grab corner (top corner, bottom corner, or middle edge, forward and backward) with a deeper, curlier 3D rolled paper cylinder and realistic crease shading.
- **Improved Pen Quick Actions:** Upgraded the stylus radial wheel with a live active-tool center hub, circular color swatches, non-dismissing Undo/Redo taps, a 1-tap Pen Nib switcher, Bookmark Page toggle, and direct access to Table, Web Page, and Sticky Note tools.
- **Better Scribble-to-Erase & Highlighter:** Tuned Scribble-to-Erase for natural quick scratch-outs across both ink strokes and individual converted-text words without false triggers during cursive writing; upgraded the Highlighter with a cleaner flat-chisel marker feel and subtle horizontal line snapping for neat, level highlights.
- **Hold-to-Shape Live Adjust & Slimmer Black Lasso:** Continue holding after Hold-to-Shape snaps a stroke to resize, rotate, or re-aim the shape live before lifting; slimmed black lasso outlines and selection handles for cleaner precision.
- **Handwriting Copy & Upgraded Tapes:** Copy editable vector handwriting across pages and notebooks or copy handwriting as recognized text; added Rectangle tape mode alongside Straight and Freehand, fixed blurry custom tapes with crisp high-res tiling, and permanently saved imported custom tapes inside the app.
- **Cloud Storage (Google Drive, OneDrive & iCloud Drive):** Store full library backups, link synced cloud folders for automatic mirroring, restore archives, and open/import documents directly from Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Apple iCloud Drive.
- **Smoother Notebook Creation & Lag-Free Dropdowns:** Eliminated upfront CPU thumbnail spikes and title-typing recompositions in the New Notebook dialog, and optimized dropdown/popover animations across the app.
